# Editing English prose

Use the target reader and the writer's sample to choose register. Natural English may be formal, technical, conversational, or literary. Do not automatically add slang, contractions, first-person anecdotes, or deliberate errors.

## Decisions that matter

- Replace inflated importance with supported content. If a sentence describes a result, retain the result and its scope; do not manufacture a measurement to make it vivid.
- Review clusters of prestige language such as “pivotal,” “transformative,” and metaphorical “landscape.” Keep a term that carries a field-specific meaning or a supported judgment.
- Prefer a simple verb when a longer phrase merely restates it. Keep functional verbs and established technical constructions when they express something distinct.
- Inspect clause tails that announce what a fact supposedly symbolizes. Keep an actual consequence; remove empty interpretation. Do not turn sequence or correlation into causation.
- Preserve useful hedges and passives. “May reduce error” and “samples were frozen” can be exactly right. Remove duplicated hedging only when the remaining wording preserves uncertainty.
- Examine repeated openings, mirrored paragraph shapes, staged candid hooks, stock objections, and generic conclusions. Change the underlying construction rather than just swapping its punctuation.
- Restore the relationship between short clauses when it is supported: condition, cause, concession, or evidence. Also retain a deliberate short sentence when its emphasis is earned.
- Keep valid groups of three, purposeful antithesis, necessary lists, and genuine asides. There is no universal dash quota or sentence-length target.
- Match the publication's spelling, punctuation, quotation style, headings, and formatting. Preserve quotations, code, names, citation keys, units, and link targets.

## Original worked example

Source: “The release marks a transformative milestone in our journey. It adds offline reading. Notes still require a connection, and syncing may take up to 30 seconds after reconnecting.”

Edit: “The release adds offline reading. Notes still require a connection, and syncing may take up to 30 seconds after reconnecting.”

The edit removes a promotional judgment while retaining the feature, limitation, maximum delay, and triggering condition. It does not claim that syncing is immediate or that offline editing is available.

## Return the requested deliverable

Do the editing pass internally. Return the finished text when requested, a critique when requested, or an edited file within the authorized scope. Do not append an assistant greeting, an invitation to continue, or an AI-authorship score to standalone copy.
